# Code of conduct

This project is a place to work on a tool, and everyone who takes part in it, in issues, pull requests and reviews, is expected to keep it a place where others can do that too.

## What is expected

- Be respectful. Disagree with what is written, not with who wrote it.
- Assume the other person means well, and ask before assuming they do not.
- Keep to the subject: the code, the documentation, the problem at hand.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change, and say why you disagree if you do.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, personal attacks, and remarks about a person's origin, gender, age, body, religion, disability or orientation.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private, including continuing to contact someone who has asked you to stop.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or a recording they are in, without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery.

## What happens

A maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that go against this, and may block a person from the project, for a time or for good.

## Reporting

The maintainer is [@Mariotutal](https://github.com/Mariotutal). This project has no private channel of its own yet: an issue or a comment that mentions the maintainer is public. To report something without making it public, use the "Report content" option on the comment or issue, which goes to GitHub and not to the maintainer; see [Reporting abuse or spam](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
